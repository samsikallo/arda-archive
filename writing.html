The Scriptorium

Write in Tolkien’s scripts — every glyph drawn by this archive, every value marked with whose evidence it is

This page writes. It does not translate. The Cirth below are the archive’s own drawings of all sixty certar, and each value carries a marker: green where Appendix E states it in prose, plain where it is read off the Angerthas plate — the shape is Tolkien’s, the reading is ours. Where plate and prose disagree, both are shown and neither is adopted, and four of those disagreements are Tolkien’s own: Appendix E says the assignment of s and h “continued to hesitate” between arrangements.

The tengwar transcriber, the on-screen keyboard, the glosser and the merged lexicon remain in tongues & letters. They are working panels and moving one is its own change, measured on its own.
