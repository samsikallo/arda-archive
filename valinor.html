Aman

drawn from the corpus — the archive's own work, not Tolkien's
This map is ours, and that is the point. Every other map in this archive traces Christopher Tolkien's own linework. There is no plate of his hand for Aman — he never drew one, and his father's Ambarkanta diagrams show the world in section, not this coast. So this sheet could not be traced. It was constructed from what the texts say, and a construction is the archive's own work. The 26 features below each carry the passage that places them; what the corpus gives is always a relation — north of, behind, in the midst of — and never a coordinate. The coordinates are ours: one solution to those relations, not a discovery. Another hand satisfying the same constraints would draw a different coast.

Named, and nowhere placed

These 2 are not on the map, and their absence is the most careful thing on this page. The corpus names them and never says where they are. A map that put them somewhere would be inventing Tolkien, which this archive does not do.

  • the gardens of Lórien — 'in the land of the Valar' is the whole of it. Lórien is placed nowhere within Valinor, and the lake of Lórellin is placed only within Lórien
    “In Lórien are his gardens in the land of the Valar, and they are the fairest of all places in the world” — t1_a_the_silmarillion.txt:2334
  • the woods of Oromë — Oromë's woods in Aman are named but never sited; what the corpus places is his riding in Middle-earth, which is a different thing
    “Oromë loved the lands of Middle-earth, and he left them unwillingly” — t1_a_the_silmarillion.txt:2376
