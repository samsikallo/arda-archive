The Plate Catalogue

Twenty-two map plates this archive holds — who drew each one, which book printed it, what it covers, and who owns it until when. No plate is reproduced here, and the reason is stated rather than implied.

This page is the catalogue, not the plates. Of the twenty-two, one is the owner’s own work and is already published as the archive’s base map. The other twenty-one are in copyright, and no licence to this archive exists anywhere in its tree — every row below names the rightsholder and the year the term runs to.

The archive’s standing rule on these is three words: measure, don’t serve. Their geometry is fact and may be measured freely; their expression is not the archive’s to redraw as its own. So what a reader gets here is the record — what exists, who made it, and who holds it — which is the half that carries no permission problem at all.

Recording who made a thing and being allowed to reproduce it are different questions. Attribution is not a licence, and an author who cannot be identified is not an author who is absent.
