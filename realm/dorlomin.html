Dor-lómin (House of Hador)

vassal lordship · Edain (Third House) · F.A. c.416–495 (free); occupied to 587

Its bounds

Southern Hithlum under Ered Wethrin; Amon Darthir above Húrin's fields; cold, misted, horse-rearing country.a

Its governance

Hereditary lordship under the High King: Hador → Galdor → Húrin (with Huor); after the Nirnaeth, Brodda and the incomer Easterlings rule by seizure — occupation government shown in detail in the Narn (thralls, seized women, the hall-order of Brodda).b

Its rulers

Hador, Galdor, Húrin — 'mightiest of the warriors of mortal Men'; Brodda the usurper by force.c
