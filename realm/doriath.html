Doriath

kingdom (hidden) · Sindar · Y.T. – F.A. 506

Its bounds

The forests of Neldoreth and Region about the river Esgalduin, with Nivrim west of Sirion and Aelin-uial of the Twilight Meres; fenced by the Girdle of Melian, 'an unseen wall of shadow and bewilderment'.a

Its history

Eldest of the realms of Beleriand: from the Sleep of Yavanna through the whole First Age it stands unconquered by Morgoth; it falls not to Angband but to Dwarven vengeance and the Oath of Fëanor.b

Its governance

Absolute kingship of Elu Thingol with Melian as counsellor-queen ('the counsel of Melian' the realm's recurring hinge); marchwardens under captains such as Beleg on the borders; Mablung chief captain within.c
