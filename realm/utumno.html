Utumno

dark fortress-realm · Melkor's host · before the Trees – Y.T. 1099

1

Its bounds

Beneath Ered Engrin in the uttermost North, its pits never wholly cleansed — 'the Valar... did not discover all the mighty vaults'.

The archive's reading

[C]

2

Its history

The first tyranny: from it the Spring of Arda is marred, and in its dungeons (the corpus's dread hypothesis, given as the wise's opinion, not fact) captive Quendi may have been ruined into Orcs.

[I-H]

Why this is a register entry

A whole spread is earned by having an illustration — the owner's rule of 19 August. Here, no illustration is bound to it, so it is an entry in a register spread Ruled space, left blank: the archive fills it when a plate is found, and this entry becomes a spread of its own.
