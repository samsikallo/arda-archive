Utumno

dark fortress-realm · Melkor's host · before the Trees – Y.T. 1099

Its bounds

Beneath Ered Engrin in the uttermost North, its pits never wholly cleansed — 'the Valar... did not discover all the mighty vaults'.a

Its history

The first tyranny: from it the Spring of Arda is marred, and in its dungeons (the corpus's dread hypothesis, given as the wise's opinion, not fact) captive Quendi may have been ruined into Orcs.b
