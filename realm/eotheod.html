The Éothéod

tribal principality · Northmen · T.A. c.1856–2510

Its bounds

The vales between Greylin and Anduin under the Grey Mountains; Framsburg at the rivers' meet.a

Its governance

Lords of one house (Frumgar, Fram, later Léod, Eorl); horse-tribute and dragon-feud their recorded politics.b

What the corpus says

“Gondor, and Eorl, Lord of the Éothéod, after the Battle of the Field of Celebrant was over and”c

“Éothéod exchange vows of perpetual friendship and alliance. We listen with”d

“of Éothéod near the sources of Anduin only some five hundred years before”e

The name stands in 151 cited lines, in 15 of the corpus's 192 texts — most often in Unfinished Tales of Numenor and Middle Earth (55), The Complete Tolkien Companion (36), The Complete Guide to Middle Earth (25).f

The drafts against the published text

16 cited lines stand in 2 volumes of the drafts and 55 in 1 of the published books — the published text carries it more often than the drafts did.g
