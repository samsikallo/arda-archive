Ladros & the Bëorian lands

vassal lordship · Edain (First House) · F.A. c.410–460

Its bounds

The north-eastern slopes of Dorthonion; earlier Estolad ('the Encampment') in East Beleriand was the First House's landless first home.a

Its governance

Lordship under Finrod's house: Boromir, Bregor, Bregolas and Barahir named lords; after the burning, Barahir's twelve companions are an outlaw-fellowship whose names the corpus preserves entire.b

Its people

The First House 'dark or brown of hair, but fair of face, with grey eyes'; from it come Beren, and through Beren all the Half-elven lines; the ring of Barahir, Finrod's oath-token, becomes the longest-lived heirloom of Men.c
