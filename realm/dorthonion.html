Dorthonion

lordship · Noldor (house of Finarfin) & Bëorians · F.A. 7–455

Its bounds

High pine-clad highland between the passes of Sirion and Aglon, rising by gentle slopes from Ard-galen and falling southward in dreadful precipices towards Doriath; Tarn Aeluin its hallowed lake.a

Its governance

Twin lordship of Angrod and Aegnor under Finrod; Ladros fief to the Bëorians within it.b
