Eregion

craft-republic under lords · Noldor · S.A. 750–1697

Its bounds

The holly-lands between Glanduin and Bruinen under the western faces of the Misty Mountains, gated to Khazad-dûm's West-door.a

Its governance

A polity of the guild: the Gwaith-i-Mírdain, People of the Jewel-smiths, come to overshadow their own lords — UT records an actual coup: the Mírdain 'seized power' from Galadriel and Celeborn under Annatar's sway, with Celebrimbor their master.b

Its living

Craft-economy fed by Moria's mithril; its masterworks the Rings themselves and the Doors of Durin ('Celebrimbor of Hollin drew these signs') — the one realm whose exports govern all later history.c
