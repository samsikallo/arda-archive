Arnor

kingdom · Dúnedain of the North · S.A. 3320 – T.A. 861

Its bounds

All Eriador between Lhûn and Bruinen from the Gwathló to the Ice-bay at its height: Annúminas on Evendim, Fornost on the North Downs, the Tower Hills westward, Amon Sûl's watchtower its heart of union.a

Its history

The elder realm and the frailer: 'the Dúnedain of the North... dwindled'; its story after division is the long defeat against Angmar — the kingdom's true fall is demographic before it is military.b

Its governance

Elendil High King over both kingdoms (Gondor held 'in common' by his sons); the palantíri its instruments of state — Annúminas, Amon Sûl and Elostirion the northern three.c
