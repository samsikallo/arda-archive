The Reunited Kingdom

high kingdom · Dúnedain and all free peoples in federation · T.A. 3019 / F.o.A. – enduring

Its history

Annúminas rebuilt, the White Tree found and planted, Minas Ithil's vale left clean and empty, the northern waste re-peopled — the Fourth Age's opening is told as restoration-catalogue.a

Its governance

High Kingship over both realms with subordinate princedoms (Dol Amroth, Ithilien), a sworn free ally (Rohan), chartered autonomies (the Shire, Drúadan) and emancipated grants (Núrn) — the corpus's final constitutional settlement, given in the appendices' own summary.b
