Rohan

kingdom · Rohirrim (Éothéod) · T.A. 2510 – enduring

Its bounds

The green plains between Misty Mountains, Isen, White Mountains and Anduin's Wolds; Edoras under the Starkhorn, Helm's Deep and Dunharrow the mountain-refuges; the Entwash's vales its wealth.a

Its governance

War-kingship of the House of Eorl in two Lines (the Marshalls of the Mark the great offices; Erkenbrand, Elfhelm named); the golden hall Meduseld its court; succession by kin-choice at need (Fréaláf sister-son, Éomer sister-son again — the pattern is canonical).b

Its people

Anglo-Saxon-figured horse-culture: the alliterative laments ('Where now the horse and the rider?'), mound-burial in two lines of barrows under simbelmynë, the horns of the Mark, oath-culture from Eorl's Oath to Éowyn's charge; Wormtongue the study of counsel corrupted.c
