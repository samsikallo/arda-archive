Bree-land

free village-league · Men & Hobbits together · Second Age origins – enduring

Its bounds

Four villages about Bree-hill at the meeting of the Great East Road and the Greenway — a crossroads-state in miniature, hedge and dike its whole wall.a

Its governance

No lord named ever: gate-wardens, inn-custom and common opinion — 'the Big Folk and the Little Folk... on friendly terms, minding their own affairs in their own ways' — the corpus's working model of stateless coexistence.b

Its living

The Prancing Pony as economic institution: news, lodging, the pony-trade (12 silver pennies extortion documented); Greenway traffic its livelihood and its danger.c
