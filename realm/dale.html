Dale

city-kingdom · Northmen (Bardings) · before 2770; refounded T.A. 2944 – enduring

Its bounds

The town in the Mountain's southern vale on the Running River, between Erebor's arms; Esgaroth its lake-partner downstream.a

Its living

The corpus's happiest market: the toy market of Dale was 'the wonder of the North', corn and provisions up from the lake for the Dwarves' craft — a described symbiosis restored after 2944 (the friendship of the Mountain and the Men of Dale).b
