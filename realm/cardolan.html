Cardolan

kingdom (failing) · Dúnedain & lesser Men · T.A. 861–1636

Its bounds

Minhiriath and the downs: Tyrn Gorthad (the Barrow-downs) its hallowed height, Tharbad its river-town shared with the south.a

Its history

Contested Weathertop with Arthedain, bled white by 1409, finished by plague — its tombs outlived it, and one dagger of its armouries reached the Witch-king's undoing.b
