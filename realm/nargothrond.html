Nargothrond

hidden city-kingdom · Noldor & Sindar · F.A. 52–495

Its bounds

The caves above Narog at the doors of the Ringwil, behind the guarded plain Talath Dirnen; the realm ran from Nenning to Sirion's west bank, greatest in extent of the Noldor lands.a

Its history

Founded on Doriath's model; long the strength of the west; policy breaks between secrecy and the open war urged by Túrin — the bridge over Narog is the realm's self-inflicted wound.b

Its governance

Kingship with a council that can overrule the king in fact: Celegorm and Curufin sway 'the most part of the people' against Finrod, who casts down his crown; under Orodreth, Túrin governs war-policy without title — though he would not take the crown.c
