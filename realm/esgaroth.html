Esgaroth

merchant republic · Northmen · old; standing through T.A. – enduring

Its governance

The corpus's one true republic: the Master, who was elected from among the old and wise — explicitly contrasted with kingship ('we have always elected masters from among the old and wise, and have not endured the rule of mere fighting men'); public opinion and song sway policy on the quays.a

Its living

Entrepôt of the North: wine-trade to the Elvenking (the barrels!), Dorwinion vintages, tolls of the lake — commerce is the constitution.b
