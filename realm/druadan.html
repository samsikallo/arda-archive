The Drúedain

tribal people · Drúedain · First Age – enduring

Its governance

Headmanship: 'great headman, Ghân-buri-Ghân', counting 'many things: stars in sky, leaves on trees, men in the dark'; treaty made orally and kept exactly.a

Its people

The watchful stones, the poisoned darts feared by orcs, the drum-language of the eaves; UT's essay is a full ethnography — the one 'primitive' people the corpus treats with complete anthropological seriousness.b
