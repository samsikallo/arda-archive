Dol Guldur

dark fortress-realm · Nazgûl, orcs; the Necromancer · T.A. c.1050–3019

1

Its bounds

The bare hill Amon Lanc, southern Mirkwood — Oropher's first seat become the Enemy's; its shadow the wood's sickness for two millennia.

The archive's reading

[I-H]

2

Its governance

Sauron's deniable capital: the corpus's study in strategic ambiguity — an unnamed 'Necromancer' whose identity the Wise debate for eight centuries while the pits hold prisoners of state (Thráin, the ring taken).

Why this is a register entry

A whole spread is earned by having an illustration — the owner's rule of 19 August. Here, no illustration is bound to it, so it is an entry in a register spread Ruled space, left blank: the archive fills it when a plate is found, and this entry becomes a spread of its own.
