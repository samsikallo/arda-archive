Dol Guldur

dark fortress-realm · Nazgûl, orcs; the Necromancer · T.A. c.1050–3019

Its bounds

The bare hill Amon Lanc, southern Mirkwood — Oropher's first seat become the Enemy's; its shadow the wood's sickness for two millennia.a

Its governance

Sauron's deniable capital: the corpus's study in strategic ambiguity — an unnamed 'Necromancer' whose identity the Wise debate for eight centuries while the pits hold prisoners of state (Thráin, the ring taken).b
