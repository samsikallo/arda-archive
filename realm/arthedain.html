Arthedain

kingdom · Dúnedain of the North · T.A. 861–1974

Its bounds

Between Lhûn and Weather Hills (Baranduin its south-line): Fornost Erain on the North Downs the king-seat, Annúminas already forsaken; the Tower Hills and the Elostirion-stone westward.a

Its governance

Isildur's line unbroken, kings taking royal names in ar(a)-; the palantíri of Annúminas and Amon Sûl its regalia; after the fall, the same line continues as the Chieftains of the Dúnedain — kingship folded into wardenship.b
