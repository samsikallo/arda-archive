Erebor & Durin's Folk in exile

kingdom (mansion-realm) · Dwarves (Durin's Folk) · T.A. 1999 – enduring (with breaks)

Its bounds

The Lonely Mountain's halls above the Running River's spring; the Front Gate, the hidden door of the thrush; the Grey Mountains and Ered Luin chapters of the exile westward.a

Its governance

Kingship of Durin's line continued in exile ('Longbeards' senior line); the burned-Dwarf rule after Azanulbizar; the Arkenstone as crown-jewel with juridical force (Thorin's bargaining, its burial on his breast).b

Its living

The Mountain's veins and the toy-market of Dale; the exile's poverty told plainly (iron... coal... they prospered in a fashion); the hoard itemized (the Arkenstone, the mithril-coat, Girion's emeralds) — treasure as politics throughout.c
