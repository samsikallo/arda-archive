The house of Tom Bombadil

anomalous domain · — · 'Eldest' – enduring

1

Its bounds

The Old Forest and the Downs between Buckland's hedge and the Barrow-country — a bounded march older than every realm around it.

The archive's reading

[I-H]

2

Its governance

'The Master of wood, water and hill' who owns nothing and commands everything within bounds he will not cross ('Tom has his house to mind, and Goldberry is waiting') — sovereignty without possession, the Council's own puzzle ('power to defy our Enemy is not in him, unless such power is in the earth itself').

Why this is a register entry

A whole spread is earned by having an illustration — the owner's rule of 19 August. Here, no illustration is bound to it, so it is an entry in a register spread Ruled space, left blank: the archive fills it when a plate is found, and this entry becomes a spread of its own.
