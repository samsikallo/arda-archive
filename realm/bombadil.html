The house of Tom Bombadil

anomalous domain · — · 'Eldest' – enduring

Its bounds

The Old Forest and the Downs between Buckland's hedge and the Barrow-country — a bounded march older than every realm around it.a

Its governance

'The Master of wood, water and hill' who owns nothing and commands everything within bounds he will not cross ('Tom has his house to mind, and Goldberry is waiting') — sovereignty without possession, the Council's own puzzle ('power to defy our Enemy is not in him, unless such power is in the earth itself').b
