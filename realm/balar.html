The Isle of Balar & the Havens of Sirion

refuge polity · Elves of every realm & Edain remnant · F.A. 473–587

Its bounds

The great isle in Balar's bay and the delta-shore at Sirion's mouths — sea-fenced remnant-lands under Ulmo's hand.a

Its governance

Double leadership: Círdan's ships, the young Gil-galad's kingship-in-refuge; at Sirion, Elwing and the Silmaril as rallying-token — sovereignty reduced to a jewel and a name.b
