Khazad-dûm

kingdom (mansion-realm) · Dwarves (Durin's Folk) · Y.T. – T.A. 1981 (as realm); colony 2989–94

Its bounds

Under Caradhras, Celebdil and Fanuidhol, from the East-gate above Azanulbizar to the Doors of Durin on Sirannon; the Endless Stair from deepest dungeon to Durin's Tower; Kheled-zâram the crown-mirror.a

Its history

Eldest and greatest of Dwarf-realms, unbroken from before the Sun to T.A. 1981 — 'the ruin of Beleriand' only enriched it; ended not by enemy siege but by delving too deep.b

Its governance

Kingship of Durin's line, the king also 'Durin' returned by belief seven times; guild-craft implied by the words of Gimli and the works themselves; the Book of Mazarbul shows record-keeping to the end.c
