The Beornings

chieftaincy · Northmen (skin-changer lords) · T.A. 2941 – enduring

Its governance

Personal chieftaincy by prowess; toll-lordship of the crossings ('But their tolls are high', Glóin grumbles) — a micro-state that keeps two mountain-roads open.a

Its people

Honey-economy, beast-speech, the great wooden hall; no meat eaten — the corpus's one vegetarian polity.b
