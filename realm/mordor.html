Mordor

dark empire · Sauron's host (Orcs, trolls, thrall-Men); client Easterlings & Haradrim · S.A. c.1000 – T.A. 3019

Its bounds

Fenced by Ered Lithui and Ephel Dúath meeting at Cirith Gorgor; Gorgoroth's ash-plateau, Orodruin its forge-heart, Núrn's sad fields about the bitter inland sea southward; the Morannon, Cirith Ungol and the Morgul-pass its three doors.a

Its governance

The Eye's absolute state: Barad-dûr's hierarchy glimpsed through the Mouth of Sauron ('Lieutenant of the Tower'), Nazgûl as viceroys (Minas Morgul, Dol Guldur), rival Orc-commands (Shagrat/Gorbag's quarrel) policed by fear; numbered whips and rations in the march to the Morannon.b

Its living

Slave-agriculture: 'great slave-worked fields away south in this wide realm... by the dark sad waters of Lake Núrnen' — the corpus's one explicit answer to how an evil realm eats; mines, forges and tribute of the client-East.c
