Ossiriand

tribal territory · Laiquendi (Green-elves) · Y.T. – F.A. 587

Its bounds

Between Gelion and the Blue Mountains under the seven rivers; green, song-filled, its people unseen — 'a stranger might pass through their land from end to end and see none of them'.a

Its governance

Kingship abandoned as policy after Denethor's fall — the one canonical case of a people renouncing monarchy from grief; thereafter hidden-folk custom, hunters with bows.b

What the corpus says

“little peopled; and it was so named, the Land of Seven Rivers, because it lay”c

“for the ancient Ossiriand, or such of it as remained above the sea (see the”d

The name stands in 1,117 cited lines, in 60 of the corpus's 192 texts — most often in The Complete Tolkien Companion (131), The Lost Road and Other Writings (101), The Silmarillion (88).e
