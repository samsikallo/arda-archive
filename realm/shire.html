The Shire

autonomous halfling land · Hobbits · T.A. 1601 – enduring

Its bounds

Forty leagues from Far Downs to Brandywine Bridge, fifty from moor to marsh; the Four Farthings, Buckland beyond the river, later the Westmarch — 'a district of well-ordered business'.a

Its governance

The corpus's minimal state, described outright: 'The Shire at this time had hardly any government.' Thain (military-nominal), Mayor of Michel Delving (elected at the Free Fair every seventh year; Post and Watch under him), Master of Buckland; twelve Shirriffs and the Bounders; family-rule ('families for the most part managed their own affairs').b

Its people

Birthday mathom-giving, six meals, pipe-weed lore (the Prologue's own monograph), the Free Fair, genealogical passion — 'They love peace and quiet and good tilled earth'; postal literacy notable (the Prologue's picture of it; the wording here is the archive's).c
