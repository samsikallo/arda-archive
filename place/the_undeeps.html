Where it stands

The Anduin's great loops where Eorl crossed riding south.a

What the corpus says

“are included, such as Lond Daer, Drúwaith Iaur, Edhellond, the Undeeps, Greylin; and a few”b

“Anduin on N 13 (afterwards called the North and South Undeeps: see”c

“The Undeeps, two great westward bends in the Anduin between the”d

The name stands in 36 cited lines, in 6 of the corpus's 192 texts — most often in Unfinished Tales of Numenor and Middle Earth (18), The Lord of the Rings a Reader's Companion (8), The Treason of Isengard (4).e
