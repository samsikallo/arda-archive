Isengard

Orthanc; Nan Curunír · TA

Where it stands

Orthanc in the Ring of Isengard. 'Unbreakable tower' is the Atlas's phrase (t3) and occurs nowhere in LotR; the Silmarillion says they made the Pinnacle of Orthanc of unbreakable STONE, and LotR III.8 says the tower stood 'unbroken by the storm' — an outcome, not a property. The Ents reached it on the night of 2 March and App B closes the event on the 3rd: 'Ents complete the destruction of Isengard'. 5 March is Théoden's arrival and the parley.a
