Udûn

SA · TA

Where it stands

“Utumno, Sindarin Udûn (Gandalf in Moria named the Balrog ‘Flame of Udûn’), a name afterwards used of the deep dale in Mordor between the Morannon and the Isenmouthe.” — The Silmarillion [C] Christopher Tolkien letters UDUN on the General Map between the Morannon and the Isenmouthe; NOT Utumno, which shares the Sindarin namea
