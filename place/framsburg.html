Where it stands

A name Tolkien gave for the Baynes map, near Éothéod; the Reader's Companion (t2) says 'presumably a stronghold named for Fram' — the hedge is its own; the Complete Tolkien Companion (t3) asserts it flatly, and they are different books. No narrative text places it.a
