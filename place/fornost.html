Fornost

Norbury; Deadmen's Dike · TA

1

Where it stands

The North-kingdom's last capital; the great battle that broke Angmar was fought west of it, on the plain between Nenuial and the North Downs (App A I(iv), undated there) and is dated 1975 by App B — App A gives only 1974, the year Angmar TOOK Fornost.

The text

App A I(iii-iv) [C]

2

By another name

The archive's reading

[I-H]

?

Why this is a register entry

A whole spread is earned by having an illustration — the owner's rule of 19 August. Here, no illustration is bound to it, so it is an entry in a register spread Ruled space, left blank: the archive fills it when a plate is found, and this entry becomes a spread of its own.
