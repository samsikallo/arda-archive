Númenor

island, realm · SA · also Westernesse, Anadûnê, Elenna, Atalantë

Númenor — island, realm, SA. Shaped like a 'five-pointed star, or pentangle', its 'five large peninsular promontories' about the Mittalmar, with the Meneltarma at its heart; the road ran from 'Rómenna in the east, to the royal city of Armenelos' and thence to Andúnië in the west, and 'the most beautiful of all the havens of Númenor, Eldalondë the Green' lay on the Andustar. UT's 'Description' treats each region in turn — the corpus's fullest physical geography of any land. (The name Elenna is the Akallabêth's, not the Description's.) — UT, Description of Númenor [I-H] — the archive's synthesis
