Fornost Erain

ruin, city · TA · also Norbury, Deadmen's Dike

Fornost Erain — ruin, city, TA; within Arthedain. spec TA: ruined city; drawn on the vector spec's TA plate, which its Section 0 calls Third Age Middle-earth; held under two keys until they were folded: Fornost Erain, Norbury of the Kings, IS Fornost. One record has the city and the coordinate, the other the ruin it became; the place was both, in that order. [I-H]; also Norbury; Deadmen's Dike; The North-kingdom's last capital; the great battle that broke Angmar was fought west of it, on the plain between Nenuial and the North Downs (App A I(iv), undated there) and is dated 1975 by App B — App
