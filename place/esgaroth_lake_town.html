Esgaroth (Lake-town)

town, lake · TA · also Lake-town

Esgaroth (Lake-town) — town, lake, TA; within Dale. spec TA: pile-town; also Lake-town; The wooden town on huge piles (Hobbit x; 'on stilts' is Foster's word, t3). Smaug fell FULL ON THE TOWN — 'Full on the town he fell... The lake roared in' (Hobbit xiv) — not into the lake; the lake came in over the wreck. The year 2941 is App B's; The Hobbit carries no dates.; within the Dale & Erebor boundary as the living map draws it, T.A. 1999–2770; 2941– [I-M]
