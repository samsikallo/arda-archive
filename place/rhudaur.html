Where it stands

The ruins in the Trolls' wood — 'old castles with an evil look, as if they had been built by wicked people' in the Lone-lands, and 'the ruins of towers: they had an ominous look' on the road to the Ford — are most likely its remains, but neither passage names the realm: Strider says only that Men here 'fell under the shadow of Angmar'. The one place the corpus joins the two by name is Tolkien's note 'Rhudaur north of the R. Bruinen (where later were the Trollshaws)'. — Hobbit 2; LotR I.12; HoME XII [I-H] — the archive's synthesis of canon, not a quotation the realms model dates it T.A. 861–c.1409a
