The Dead Marshes — marsh, TA. The mere of Dagorlad's dead faces; Gollum led them through, Mar 1-2, 3019.; within the Gondor at its height boundary as the living map draws it, T.A. c.1050–1448 [I-M]; “He then wins the Battle of the Camp, and drives Wainriders into the Dead Marshes.” — LotR App B, T.A. 1944 [C]; LotR II.8 [C]; the dead of the Battle of Dagorlad lie in it; the Noman-lands stand between it and Cirith Gorgor, so it is not before the Gate — Celeborn: the Emyn Muil 'look out over the Dead Marshes and the Noman-lands to Cirith Gorgor and the black gates of Mordor' (LotR II.8 [C]).
