The Index

Every name the volumes themselves indexed, with the editor's own statement of what it is — and where two editors both wrote one, both are kept
6,630 headwords, 16,316 glosses, from the indexes of 14 volumes. These are not page references. Christopher Tolkien's indexes carry, in his own words, a short statement concerning each person and place — so most entries here are a definition written by the editor who knew the material best, and for many names it is the only one that exists. 2,721 headwords are glossed by two or more volumes, and every gloss is kept. Two editors differing is evidence, not a conflict to resolve; each gloss names the book that wrote it, and that naming is its citation.
