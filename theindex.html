The Index

Every name the volumes themselves indexed, with the editor's own statement of what it is — and where two editors both wrote one, both are kept
6,343 names, drawn from the indexes 14 volumes wrote for themselves. These are not page-references with a name attached — Christopher Tolkien's indexes carry, in his own words, a short statement concerning each person and place, so most entries here are a definition written by the editor who knew the material best, and for many names it is the only one that exists. Where several volumes describe the same name, every statement is kept and attributed: identical wordings once, a statement wholly contained in a fuller one folded into it, and genuine disagreements side by side. Nothing is paraphrased. 2,463 names appear in two or more of the indexes.
