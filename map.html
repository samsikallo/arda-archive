The Map of Arda

every named feature the archive draws, and every one of them answers

Hover or focus a feature and its ink turns red; click it for the record. A feature drawn dashed is one whose placing the archive infers rather than reads — the name may still be Tolkien's own. 234 features are drawn here; 217 answer to a record in the gazetteer, and the rest are the draughtsman's own labels. The roads are placed by the place each stage's own label names — never by transforming another map's coordinates onto this one, which measurement showed to be forty-four miles out. A stage the archive cannot place is not drawn, and a course is broken where it cannot be followed: 71 of 88 stages are placed. The chronicle moves through the moments the archive actually records — a place is faint before it was made and dashed after it was thrown down — and a place it cannot date is left alone, because dimming it would assert an absence no text states.

This sheet is a palimpsest: Beleriand is drawn as it was, beneath the pale water that now covers it. For the political extent of the realms age by age — which is drawn in another frame this archive has measured and will not force onto this one — see the turning of the Ages and the living map.
