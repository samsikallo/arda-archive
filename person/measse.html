etymology

  • Meássë is a Qenya name, deriving from the root MEHE ("ooze?") and related to the word mear ("gore"). Its Gnomish equivalent is Mechos or Mechothli. Among her other names was Rávi (related to ravennë "she-lion")., as well as Tarkil, deriving from the root TARA ("to batter, thud, beat").

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Book of Lost Tales Part One, Appendix: Names in the Lost Tales - Part I, p. 260; J.R.R. Tolkien, "Early Qenya and The Valmaric Script", in Parma Eldalamberon XIV (edited by Carl F. Hostetter, Christopher Gilson, Arden R. Smith, Patrick H. Wynne, and Bill Welden), p. 15
