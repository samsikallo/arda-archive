  • Lothíriel married Éomer, King of Rohan, in . They had at least one son, Elfwine the Fair, who became the 19th King of Rohan upon the death of his father in . Whether Lothíriel still lived by that date, or died after, is unknown.

other versions

  • In the published version of The Return of the King, Lothíriel and Éomer were wedded in "the last year of the Third Age". In Appendix B, "The Tale of Years" states that 3021 was the last of the Third Age, and thus the wedding took place in that year. However, in a late manuscript of what would later become Appendix B the wedding of Lothíriel and Éomer was set in "3022 (or Fourth Age 1)".

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix B, "The Chief Days from the Fall of Barad-dûr to the End of the Third Age", p. 1096; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Peoples of Middle-earth, "VIII. The Tale of Years of the Third Age", manuscript T 4, p. 244

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Lothíriel”, revid 425861 (2025-10-15T08:04:01Z)
