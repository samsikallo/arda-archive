etymology

  • Mablung was named after Mablung of the Heavy Hand, a great Hunter of Doriath. The name is from Doriathrin mab ("hand") + lung ("heavy"). His epithet was a translation of his name

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The War of the Ring, "Faramir"; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, Part Three: "The Etymologies"; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Index"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)”, revid 406431 (2024-08-23T05:42:17Z)

history

  • Mablung's family had lived in Ithilien before it was overrun by the forces of Sauron. As such, he was given a place among the Rangers of Ithilien, Denethor's most trusted forayers.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Two Towers, "Of Herbs and Stewed Rabbit"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)”, revid 406431 (2024-08-23T05:42:17Z)

  • Together with Damrod, Anborn and several others, Mablung served under the Captain of Gondor Faramir, the Steward's second son. They were sent to intercept a troop of Southrons in March , and there, they came across Frodo Baggins and Samwise Gamgee. While standing guard over the hobbits with Damrod, he briefly conversed with them. Faramir, in the meantime, went on to ambush the Haradrim.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Two Towers, "Of Herbs and Stewed Rabbit"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)”, revid 406431 (2024-08-23T05:42:17Z)

  • Mablung and Damrod remained the guards of the Ring-bearer during their short stay at Henneth Annûn.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Two Towers, "The Window on the West"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)”, revid 406431 (2024-08-23T05:42:17Z)

  • With his Lord injured, Mablung was given command of the Rangers after the Battle of the Pelennor Fields. When the Army of the West marched on the Morannon, Mablung and his troops scouted the road and discovered an ambush by Orcs and Easterlings that lay hid in Ithilien. The ambush was quickly turned.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Return of the King, "The Black Gate Opens"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)”, revid 406431 (2024-08-23T05:42:17Z)

language

  • Westron, Sindarin

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Two Towers, "Of Herbs and Stewed Rabbit"
