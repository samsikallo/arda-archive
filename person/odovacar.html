etymology

  • J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin. The name Odovacar contains aud ("wealth") and vacar ("watchful"). It was the name of a germanic king of Italy.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix F, "On Translation", p. 1135

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Odovacar Bolger”, revid 440392 (2026-07-19T15:21:04Z)

history

  • Odovacar was the son of Herugar Bolger and Jessamine Boffin. He became head of the family after his father's death. Odovacar married Rosamunda Took, and had two children: Fredegar and Estella. They were all present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix C, "Bolger of Budgeford"
