history

  • Nori's parentage is unknown, although he was a remote kinsman of Thorin and a member of the House of Durin. He was also the brother of Dori, and a cousin of Ori. His birth year is also unknown.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "Durin's Folk"; J.R.R. Tolkien, John D. Rateliff (ed.), "Addendum: The Seventh Phase: iv. Personae", The History of The Hobbit, 2011

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nori”, revid 432025 (2026-01-27T22:03:11Z)

  • However, in Bilbo Baggins' account of the quest to Erebor he stated that the brothers Fíli and Kíli were the youngest of the thirteen "by some fifty years", and that when Thorin was missing Balin was "the eldest left". Since Kíli was born in and Balin was born in , this would put Nori's birth between and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old during the quest to Erebor.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Over Hill and Under Hill"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Barrels Out of Bond"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "Durin's Folk"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nori”, revid 432025 (2026-01-27T22:03:11Z)

  • Nori lived a wealthy life after the quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain. His date of death is unknown but he was still alive during the War of the Ring.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Fellowship of the Ring, "Many Meetings"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nori”, revid 432025 (2026-01-27T22:03:11Z)

  • Nori was one of the five Dwarves in the fourth group to arrive at Bag End. He wore a purple hood. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Nori (along with Dori and Ori) played the flute. In Beorn's Halls, when Gandalf was telling the story of the company's adventures to Beorn, Nori and Ori were the second two Dwarves to show up. Nori fought in and survived the Battle of Five Armies. He was given his share of the treasure by Dáin Ironfoot, and made his home in Erebor alongside with Dori and Ori.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "An Unexpected Party"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Queer Lodgings"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "The Return Journey"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nori”, revid 432025 (2026-01-27T22:03:11Z)
