etymology

  • Arveleg is a Sindarin name. Its meaning its not glossed, but David Salo and the Association Tolkiendil suggest that Arveleg means "Mighty King". David Salo, the Association Tolkiendil and Paul Strack suggests that it is a combination of ar(a)- ("royal", "noble", "high") and a lenited form veleg of beleg ("mighty", "great").

history

  • After his death, Arveleg was succeeded by his 18-year old son, Araphor. Though this was a young age for one of Númenórean blood, he was valiant. With the help of the Círdan of Lindon, he repelled attacks on Fornost and the North Downs.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Eriador, Arnor, and the Heirs of Isildur"
