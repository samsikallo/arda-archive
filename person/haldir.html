etymology

  • Haldir is clearly Sindarin, but it is never glossed. In the earlier legendarium, Tolkien glossed Noldorin Haldir as "Hidden Hero", though this Haldir refers to the (later omitted) son of Orodreth.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, Part Three: "The Etymologies", entry"SKAL¹"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Haldir”, revid 398696 (2024-07-06T23:38:14Z)

history

  • Haldir was accompanied by his brothers between 15 and 16 January, when they encountered the Fellowship of the Ring who arrived in Lórien. His brothers interacted little with the Fellowship because they spoke little of the Common Speech. He treated Gimli rather poorly due to bad relations between Elves and Dwarves. He made him go blindfolded through the wood until Galadriel said he did not have to.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix B, "The Great Years"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Haldir”, revid 398696 (2024-07-06T23:38:14Z)

  • He had heard of Aragorn, but there is no indication that they had met before. Haldir became their guide to the city of Caras Galadhon.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Fellowship of the Ring, "Lothlórien"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Haldir”, revid 398696 (2024-07-06T23:38:14Z)

  • After Celeborn and Galadriel had nourished their guests, and they were set to depart, Haldir led them to their Elven boats.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Fellowship of the Ring, "Farewell to Lórien"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Haldir”, revid 398696 (2024-07-06T23:38:14Z)
