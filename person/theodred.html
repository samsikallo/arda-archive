etymology

  • Théodred is probably a combination of the Anglo-Saxon words þēod ("people", "land") and rǣd ("counsel", "advice"). The word þēod also appears in the name Éothéod.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "Cirion and Eorl and the Friendship of Gondor and Rohan", "Notes", note 36

history

  • While Gríma gained more influence on the King, he considered Théodred and Éomer his chief opponents, and tried to bring the two cousins into discredit with Théoden. But their loyalty remained steadfast even in his apparent dotage; Gríma therefore tried to play them one against the other in the mind of Théoden, representing Éomer as ever eager to increase his authority, at the expense of Théodred.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "The Battles of the Fords of Isen"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Théodred”, revid 440646 (2026-08-01T20:07:45Z)

  • In the beginning of , due to the threat of invasion by the forces of the wizard Saruman, Théodred established himself at Helm's Deep to command the military strength of the Westfold. When actual war began, Théodred added to the Muster of Westfold by summoning the Muster of Edoras.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "The Battles of the Fords of Isen", Appendix (i)

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Théodred”, revid 440646 (2026-08-01T20:07:45Z)

  • Théodred was warned by his scouts of a mustering of troops at the Gate of Isengard and he reinforced the approaches. He passed over the Isen with the main strength of his cavalry to overthrow Saruman's apparent army before it was fully prepared. He scattered the vanguard, but then discovered the main host prepared for assault and ordered a retreat while the rearguard under Grimbold had to turn and drive back their pursuers.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "The Battles of the Fords of Isen"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Théodred”, revid 440646 (2026-08-01T20:07:45Z)

  • Elfhelm arrived to his support and they lifted the body. Théodred still breathed, only long enough to speak his last words. The news of Théodred's death caused Erkenbrand of Westfold to assume command of the West-mark. With no descendants or siblings, this made his cousin, Éomer, heir to the throne of Rohan.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "The Battles of the Fords of Isen"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Théodred”, revid 440646 (2026-08-01T20:07:45Z)
