etymology

  • Arvedui is Sindarin for "Last King", being a compound of the prefix ar(a)- ("noble", "royal", "high") and the lenited form of medui ("last").

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion", paragraphs about the time after the death of king Ondoher,"Arvedui was indeed the last king, as his name signifies."

history

  • In order to vanquish the evils of Angmar, Eärnur, Círdan and Glorfindel captained a combined host from Gondor, Lindon and Rivendell and marched across Eriador in such a great force that not a single Orc was left to the west of the Misty Mountains.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Arvedui”, revid 404762 (2024-08-23T02:04:39Z)

  • Arvedui's name meant "Last King" in Sindarin, and he was named so due to a prophecy by Malbeth the Seer spoken to his father at Arvedui's birth

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Arvedui”, revid 404762 (2024-08-23T02:04:39Z)

  • Arvedui married Fíriel, the daughter of King Ondoher of Gondor, in , repairing the link between the two Realms in Exile after years of estrangement. In , Ondoher, and his sons Artamir & Faramir were slain in a battle against the Wainriders. Arvedui sent messages to Gondor claiming the kingship , both as a descendant of Isildur and as the husband of Fíriel, who, under old Númenórean law, should have become the first ruling queen. In the Council of Gondor, persuaded by the Steward Pelendur, voted against Arvedui's claim in favour of Eärnil - a distant relative of the King and the victorious general in the war on the Wainriders.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Arvedui”, revid 404762 (2024-08-23T02:04:39Z)

  • thumb|Arvedui by [[:Category:Images by Steamey|Steamey]] When Arvedui became king in , his kingdom had already begun to fall apart, and was struggling desperately to hold back the forces of Angmar. In the Witch-king descended from the north, capturing Fornost and pushing most of the Dúnedain over the Lune. Arvedui and his followers rescued several heirlooms of Arnor such as the Ring of Barahir and the palantíri of Amon Sûl and Annúminas. They held out on the North Downs for a short time before being forced to hide in old dwarven mines in the Blue Mountains. In the end, due to hunger and desperation, they sought the aid of the Lossoth in the Ice-bay of Forochel.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Eriador, Arnor, and the Heirs of Isildur"
