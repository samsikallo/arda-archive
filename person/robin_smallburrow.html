history

  • Born and raised in Hobbiton, Robin was of old a friend of the Gamgees. He loved being outdoors, and became a Shirriff so he could meet new people (and, equally important, inns).

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Return of the King, "The Scouring of the Shire"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Robin Smallburrow”, revid 430238 (2026-01-12T09:59:01Z)

  • Originally one of only twelve, during Lotho Sackville-Baggins' reign, Robin found himself in over his head. He was reassigned to the First Eastfarthing Troop in Frogmorton, tasked with enforcing The Rules.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, "Prologue Concerning Hobbits, and other matters"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Robin Smallburrow”, revid 430238 (2026-01-12T09:59:01Z)

  • Robin was one of the Shirriffs sent to arrest four Hobbits who had illegally entered the Shire at the Brandywine Bridge. It was more of an escort than an arrest, as Robin and his troops were on foot and the four Hobbits had ponies. At the Three-Farthing Stone, the Shirriffs gave up and let their prisoners go on without them. It's not stated if he was among the Sheriffs who joined in the revolt.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Return of the King, "The Scouring of the Shire"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Robin Smallburrow”, revid 430238 (2026-01-12T09:59:01Z)
