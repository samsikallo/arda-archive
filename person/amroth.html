etymology

  • Amroth means "upclimber, high climber". It was probably of Silvan origin, but adapted to Sindarin. It was actually a nickname derived from his residing in a high talan or flet, as living in such lofty houses was no doubt at first thought remarkable, and Amroth was probably the first to do so.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "The History of Galadriel and Celeborn", "Amroth and Nimrodel"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix F, "The Languages and Peoples of the Third Age", "Of the Elves", footnote relating to the first paragraph
