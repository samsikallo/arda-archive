other names

  • The Gnomish cognate of Salmar is Salvor. Likewise, both Noldorin and Lirillo (another name by which he was known by) are also Qenya names: the first probably arising from his association with the Noldoli; the second meaning "Valu of the Song", containing either glîr ("a song, poem") or lir- ("sing") + masculine ending -illo. The Gnomish equivalent of Noldorin is Goldriel, and that of Lirillo is Glirlos. Another name for the character was Avlon. It is in Gnomish, but its meaning is unclear.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, "Early Qenya and The Valmaric Script", in Parma Eldalamberon XIV (edited by Carl F. Hostetter, Christopher Gilson, Arden R. Smith, Patrick H. Wynne, and Bill Welden), p. 13; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Book of Lost Tales Part One, "I. The Cottage of Lost Play": "Notes and Commentary", p. 22; J.R.R. Tolkien, "I-Lam na-Ngoldathon: The Grammar and Lexicon of the Gnomish Tongue", in Parma Eldalamberon XI (edited by Christopher Gilson, Arden R. Smith, and Patrick H. Wynne), p. 41
