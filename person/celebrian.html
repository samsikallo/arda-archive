etymology

  • Celebrían is a Sindarin name, which means "Silver queen". It is probably a compound of celeb ("silver") and rían ("queen").

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien; Humphrey Carpenter, Christopher Tolkien (eds.), The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien, Letter 345, (dated 30 November 1972), p. 423

history

  • Celebrían was born early in the Second Age, likely in , when Galadriel refused to return to Eressëa, and passed over the Mountains. Celebrían lived in Eregion, and later moved to Lórien with her mother in . In , she went to Rivendell with Galadriel searching for Celeborn, where she first met Lord Elrond, who loved her in secret. Their marriage was delayed by the wars against Sauron, though they would eventually wed in . Their first children, the twins Elladan and Elrohir, were born in , and their daughter Arwen in . She had inherited the Elfstone from her mother, which was later given to Arwen, her daughter.

  • She was followed by Elrond and her mother Galadriel, who eventually took the White Ship in the end of the Third Age and also passed the Great Sea to the Undying Lands, where they presumably reunited with her. Later Celeborn and possibly her sons, Elladan and Elrohir, also went to the Grey Havens to sail to the Undying Lands, and presumably reunited with Celebrían.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, The Return of the King, "The Grey Havens", p. 1029; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, "Prologue", "Note on the Shire Records", p. 15

other versions

  • J.R.R. Tolkien tried to define the birth-year of Celebrían in different manuscripts, specially those dealing with chronology and Elven growth. Here are the dates of the Second Age, presented in chronological order as Tolkien considered them: (circa), 300 ("probably"), 260 or , (a proposal while calculating her age), 9 (circa) or 45. In "Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn" (the only narrative dealing with a complete history of Middle-earth during the Second Age), both Celebrían and Amroth are children of Galadriel and Celeborn, but in later texts J.R.R. Tolkien gave Amroth a Sindarin origin. In the drafts of The Tale of Years of the Third Age, Elrond married Celebrían c. T.A. 2500 and their children were born in the same century. In , she was slain by Orcs instead of captured. The marriage was later changed to 2300, and her capture and departure to 2509.
