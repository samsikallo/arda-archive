history

  • Nellas was an Elf-maiden of Doriath, who lived in the woods near Menegroth. At the bidding of Melian, she watched over Túrin whenever he strayed into the woods during his boyhood years. Sometimes they played together or wandered hand in hand. To Túrin, she seemed no more than his own age.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Children of Húrin, "Túrin in Doriath", pp. 80-1

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nellas”, revid 396088 (2024-06-25T19:28:23Z)

  • It was from Nellas' teachings that Túrin learned Doriathrin, a more archaic and courteous form of Sindarin, as well as the "ways and the wild things of Doriath".

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Children of Húrin, "Túrin in Doriath", pp. 80-1

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nellas”, revid 396088 (2024-06-25T19:28:23Z)

  • Eventually however, as Túrin grew to manhood, he turned his attention to warfare, and as Nellas was unwilling to venture under the roofs of stone of Menegroth, Túrin eventually stopped meeting with her altogether. Nonetheless, Nellas continued watching over him, though now she remained hidden.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Children of Húrin, "Túrin in Doriath", pp. 80-1
