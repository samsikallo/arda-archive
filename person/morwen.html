other names

  • Her epithet, Eledhwen, means "Elfsheen" or "Elven-fair". In the early Noldorin phase, it meant "Elf-maid". J.R.R. Tolkien mentioned that the Old English adjective aelfsciene had been applied to Sarah and Judith and translated this Old English adjective as "elven-fair". She bore the title Lady of Dor-lómin, being the wife of Húrin, the Lord of Dor-lómin. In early versions of the tale, she was called "Tirannë", "Mavwin", or "Mavoine".

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Ruin of Beleriand and the Fall of Fingolfin"; J.R.R.
