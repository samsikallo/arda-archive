etymology

  • The name Bór was probably not the character's true name, as it seems to be Sindarin for "Faithful": from bôr ("steadfast; trusty man, faithful vassal"). An earlier name for the character was Bor (without the accent).

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, "Appendix: I. The Genealogies", p. 403

history

  • Bór's people came into Beleriand after the Dagor Bragollach. He and his sons Borlad, Borlach, and Borthand swore allegiance to Maedhros and Maglor. He was one of the few leaders of the Eastern Tribes of Men to remain faithful to the Eldar during the Nirnaeth Arnoediad (Battle of Unnumbered Tears).

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Ruin of Beleriand and the Fall of Fingolfin"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Bór”, revid 417144 (2025-01-19T05:03:30Z)

other versions

  • In the appendix of The Lost Road and Other Writings in The Genealogies (dating from the early 1930s), it is stated that Bor (as the character was called at that point in the legendarium) was born in the year 120.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, "Appendix: I. The Genealogies", p. 403

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Bór”, revid 417144 (2025-01-19T05:03:30Z)

  • When comparing the above date with its associated text The Earliest Annals of Beleriand, Bor was born 43 years before the Swarthy Men entered Beleriand in the year 163.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Shaping of Middle-earth, "VII. The Earliest Annals of Beleriand: [The first version of The Earliest Annals of Beleriand (Text AB I)]", entry year 163, p. 300
