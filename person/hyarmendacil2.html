etymology

  • Vinyarion is a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "New Son" and is a compound of vinya ("new") and the suffix ion ("son"). Hyarmendacil is a Quenya name. It means "South-victor". It is a compound of hyarmen ("south") and -(n)dacil ("victor").

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion", entry for king Ciryaher, p. 1045
