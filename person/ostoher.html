history

  • In , Ostoher rebuilt and enlarged Minas Anor. Upon completion, he made Minas Anor his summer residence, which became the tradition of his successors.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix B, "The Third Age"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "The Númenorean Kings", "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ostoher”, revid 406895 (2024-08-23T06:46:06Z)

  • Later, in , Gondor was invaded for the first time by Easterlings. Since Ostoher had become an old man his son Tarostar worked to defeat this threat. Tarostar succeeded, but not until , years after Ostoher's death.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix B, "The Third Age"
