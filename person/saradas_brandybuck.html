etymology

  • The meaning of the name Saradas is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style. Jim Allan suggests that the name Saradas is a variation of the name Saradoc with an -as ending like in Marmadas and Merimas. Category:Hobbits Category:Brandybuck Category:Third Age characters

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix F, "On Translation", p. 1135

history

  • Saradas was the third child and second son of Gorbadoc Brandybuck, and had one son, Seredic. He was present at Bilbo's Farewell Party.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix C, "Brandybuck of Buckland"
