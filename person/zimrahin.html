etymology

  • Zimrahin is obviously a Mannish name of the Folk of Marach. The name seems to contain the later Adûnaic element zimra "jewel", and possibly means "jewel child". Adûnaic would emerge as a descendant of the language Zimhrahin was speaking. Meldis is the Sindarin name of Zimrahin. The meaning of the name is "friend".

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, Part Three: "The Etymologies", s. v. MEL-, 372
