history

  • Almost nothing is known of Nár, other than that he accompanied Thrór, the King of Durin's folk on his final journey in his attempt to find fortune in wider world and escape the scorn of Men. They travelled together from Dunland, where Thrór's relatives and subjects were living at the time, heading north they crossed the Redhorn Pass and came down into Azanulbizar.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "Durin's Folk"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Nár”, revid 406756 (2024-08-23T06:22:43Z)

  • Nár fled back down the Silverlode to Dunland where his message caused such anger amongst his people that it raised a call to arms among all seven Dwarf Houses to avenge the elder high-king of their race, leading the War of the Dwarves and Orcs. In the climactic Battle of Azanulbizar, Azog himself was slain along with thousands of others on both sides.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "Durin's Folk"
