history

  • During his rule the Éothéod lived in the land between the Misty Mountains on the west, the Forest River in the east, the confluence of the rivers Greylin and Langwell in the south and the Grey Mountains in the north and were troubled by the Long-worm Scatha. Fram slew the Dragon, took an ancient horn, and claimed his hoard as his own. The Dwarves of that region also laid claim to the treasure, but Fram rebuked the claim, instead sending them the teeth of the Dragon along with a message. The message angered the Dwarves, and it was rumoured that they killed Fram for it.

  • The only fortified burg of the Éothéod was located at the confluence of the Greylin and the Langwell. It is possible that this burg was the settlement known as Framsburg and that it was named after Fram.
