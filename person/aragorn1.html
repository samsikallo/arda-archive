etymology

  • Aragorn's name is Sindarin, and means "Revered King", from ara, a prefix for royalty, and gorn "revered".

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, "Words, Phrases and Passages in Various Tongues in The Lord of the Rings", in Parma Eldalamberon XVII (edited by Christopher Gilson)
