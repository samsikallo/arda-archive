etymology

  • Two explanations exist for the name Uinen. In The Etymologies, the first element came from a root UY-. Several other words under this lemma mean "seaweed", though the name Uinen itself is not explained. A later note pertaining to the name instead suggests it is a name of non-Elvish (Valarin) origin of unknown meaning. Christopher Tolkien has noted that Uinen contains the element nen ("water"). It is unknown if he had access to unpublished manuscripts by his father to corroborate this suggestion or if he speculated on the matter.

history

  • Early in the history of Arda, Ossë was corrupted by Morgoth but Aulë pleaded Uinen to persuade him to return to the service of Ulmo.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Valaquenta: Of the Maiar"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Uinen”, revid 414116 (2024-12-04T23:52:48Z)

  • The Teleri of Olwë had much love for Uinen, as she and Ossë had befriended them at the river Sirion during the Great March.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of Eldamar and the Princes of the Eldalië"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Uinen”, revid 414116 (2024-12-04T23:52:48Z)

  • Uinen wept for the Teleri mariners after the kinslaying at Alqualondë and rose a storm that sunk many Noldor on the stolen Swan-ships.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Flight of the Noldor"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Uinen”, revid 414116 (2024-12-04T23:52:48Z)

  • Considered a protector and bringer of calm seas by the mariners of Númenor, they would cry to her for help, while their respect for the Valar endured. The Guild of Venturers were also known as Uinendili.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Valaquenta: Of the Maiar"; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "Aldarion and Erendis: The Mariner's Wife"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Uinen”, revid 414116 (2024-12-04T23:52:48Z)

other versions

  • In the Annals of Aman, Uinen and Ossë were originally counted among the Valar. In early versions of the legendarium, the character was also named Ónen, Solórë, or Ui (among other names), and was given the title "Queen of Mermaids".
