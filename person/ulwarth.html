history

  • After entering Beleriand, Ulwarth along with his father and his brothers joined the service of Caranthir.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Ruin of Beleriand and the Fall of Fingolfin"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • Ultimately though, his father Ulfang's purpose from the beginning was to eventually betray the Elves, which he and his brothers ultimately did in the Battle of Unnumbered Tears, under the leadership of Uldor the Accursed - they turned against their allies, attacking them in the rear. Their betrayal proved crucial in the fight against Morgoth's forces, though, and Morgoth was victorious.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The War of the Jewels, "Part One. The Grey Annals": &sect;174, pp. 60-1, footnote; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Fifth Battle: Nirnaeth Arnoediad"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • However, Ulwarth, as well as his brother Ulfast were killed by the sons of Bór, who remained loyal to the sons of Fëanor during the battle - while Ulwarth's brother Uldor was slain by Maglor.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Silmarillion, "Quenta Silmarillion: Of the Fifth Battle: Nirnaeth Arnoediad"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

other names

  • An earlier name for the character was Ulwar, afterwards changed to Ulwarth.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Shaping of Middle-earth, "VII. The Earliest Annals of Beleriand: Commentary on the Annals of Beleriand (text AB I)", entry Annal 163, p. 320

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

other versions

  • According to an earlier version of the legendarium, instead of being killed by the sons of Bór, both Ulwar (as the character was called at that time) and his brother Ulfast survived the Battle of Unnumbered Tears - in fact, they were the ones that slew Bor and his sons in battle.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, "Part Two: Valinor and Middle-earth before The Lord of the Rings, III. The Later Annals of Beleriand", entry 272, p. 137

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • Afterwards, the folk of Ulfast and Ulwar brutally oppressed the remaining native folk of Hádor who were already living there before their arrival.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, "Part Two: Valinor and Middle-earth before The Lord of the Rings, III. The Later Annals of Beleriand", entry 273, p. 138

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • In the appendix of The Lost Road and Other Writings in The Genealogies (dating from the early 1930s), it is stated that Ulwar was born in the year 130.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Lost Road and Other Writings, "Appendix: I. The Genealogies", p. 403

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • When comparing the above date with its associated text The Earliest Annals of Beleriand, Ulwar was born 33 years before the Swarthy Men entered Beleriand in the year 163.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Shaping of Middle-earth, "VII. The Earliest Annals of Beleriand: [The first version of The Earliest Annals of Beleriand (Text AB I)]", entry year 163, p. 300

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)

  • Also, the dates found in The Earliest Annals of Beleriand were by and large moved 300 years into the future in The Grey Annals (c. early 1950s), such that, for example, an event happening in the year 195 in The Earliest Annals now happened in the year .

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Shaping of Middle-earth, "VII. The Earliest Annals of Beleriand: Commentary on the Annals of Beleriand (text AB I)", Annals 155 to 157, pp. 319-20

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Ulwarth”, revid 407770 (2024-08-23T09:34:41Z)
