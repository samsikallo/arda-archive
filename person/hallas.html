history

  • Hallas was the heir of Steward Cirion, who committed the rule of Minas Tirith to his son while Cirion led an army north to fight the Balchoth in . Following the unexpected victory at the Battle of the Field of Celebrant, Hallas was a witness when Cirion gifted Calenardhon to Eorl the Young and his people. In fact, it was Hallas who devised the names Rohan and Rohirrim.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, "Cirion and Eorl and the Friendship of Gondor and Rohan"; J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), Unfinished Tales, Index
