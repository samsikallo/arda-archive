etymology

  • His name is likely derived from the Anglo-Saxon words eoh ("war-horse"), and mǣre, ("grand, excellent, famous"). Éo- sounds like "eh-ah" with the "ah" just barely pronounced. He adopted the epithet Éadig during his kingship, which is an Old English word meaning "happy, wealthy" and usually translated as "blessed", used for saints in medieval literature.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix E, "Pronunciation of Words and Names", "Note", p. 1117
