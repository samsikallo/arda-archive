history

  • Before the quest to Erebor, Fíli and Kíli probably lived in the Blue Mountains where their uncle resided after the disappearance of Thráin II. As Thorin's sister-sons, they accompanied their uncle when he set out to challenge the dragon Smaug.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "An Unexpected Party"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Kíli”, revid 432333 (2026-01-29T05:30:00Z)

  • Early on the night when the company encountered the trolls one of the ponies became frightened and bolted into the river; Kíli and Fíli nearly drowned getting the beast out of the water.

  • Up in the Misty Mountains during the thunder-battle the two brothers, being the youngest Dwarves with the keenest eyes, were sent ahead to look for shelter. They found a small dry cave which was perfect for the company (until the Goblins arrived). After Gandalf had rescued the company and was counting the members he found Kíli and Fíli, as usual, paired together.

  • When the company rushed up into trees to escape the wargs Kíli and Fíli scrambled to the top of a tall larch. The next day, when the company arrived at Beorn's hall, Kíli and Fíli were again together as the fourth pair of Dwarves to appear during Gandalf's story.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Out of the Frying-Pan into the Fire"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Queer Lodgings"

  • When Bilbo spied the boat on the far side of the Enchanted River, Fíli managed to snag it with a hook on a rope but could not pull it across. Kíli came to his aid first, followed by Óin and Glóin, before the boat came loose (after its painter snapped).

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Flies and Spiders"

  • At the opening of the barrels after the escape from the halls of the Elvenking, Kíli and Fíli came out smiling since they were young and had but few bruises. Kíli and Fíli went along with Balin and Bilbo on this expedition. On another scouting trip Bilbo was with Kíli and Fíli when the three discovered the rough steps that led up the mountain to the little bay that contained the secret door.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "A Warm Welcome"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "On the Doorstep"

Once the dragon woke up though Kíli and Fíli, along with Bilbo and Balin, were the first to go in the tunnel (sent in by Thorin).

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Inside Information"

  • While searching the treasure when the full company descended into Erebor, Kíli and Fíli were in such exuberant spirits that they took down magic golden harps and played music on them.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "Not at Home"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Kíli”, revid 432333 (2026-01-29T05:30:00Z)

  • Kíli was slain with his brother in the Battle of Five Armies, while defending their mortally wounded uncle. After Thorin's death, the brothers would have been the only remaining descendants of Thrór's line. Their deaths ended the dynasty, and the title of King under the Mountain passed to Dáin II. It was their heroic last stand that made their mother the only Dwarf-woman to be mentioned in the genealogies.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Hobbit, "The Return Journey"; J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix A, "Durin's Folk"
