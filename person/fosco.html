history

  • Fosco was the son of Largo Baggins and Tanta Hornblower. He married Ruby Bolger, and had three children: Dora, Drogo and Dudo. Fosco is the grandfather of Frodo Baggins.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix C, "Baggins of Hobbiton"
