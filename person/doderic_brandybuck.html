etymology

  • The meaning of the name Doderic is unknown. J.R.R. Tolkien noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely Celtic style. Jim Allan suggests that the name Doderic is a Germanic form of Theuderic with the variatons Theodoric and Dietrich and is a combination of the elements theud ("people") and ric ("rule", "reign").

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix F, "On Translation", p. 1135

history

  • Doderic was a son of Seredic Brandybuck and Hilda Bracegirdle. He attended Bilbo's Farewell Party.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, The Lord of the Rings, Appendix C, "Brandybuck of Buckland"

    [EXT] C? Tolkien Gateway, “Doderic Brandybuck”, revid 420318 (2025-04-25T08:01:08Z)

other versions

  • In an earlier version of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he had the name Roderic, the name of a Visigothic king of Spain between A.D. 710 and 711.

    Cited to: J.R.R. Tolkien, Christopher Tolkien (ed.), The Peoples of Middle-earth, "III. The Family Trees", manuscript BR 4
